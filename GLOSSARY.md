# Memory and principles

These terms describe the agreed purpose of the memory layers and principle library.

## Language

**L1 decision evidence**:
An attributed record of a choice, correction, reason, tradeoff, or outcome in its original context.
_Avoid_: A personal principle inferred from every task instruction.

**L2 working principle**:
A reusable way to approach a class of decisions, with an explanation and limits.
One meaningful judgment can support a principle when its reusable reasoning and boundaries are clear.
_Avoid_: A task transcript, a compulsory procedure, a preliminary core value.

**Case**:
A specific decision or experimental arrangement in its original context, which can illustrate or support a principle.
_Avoid_: A general rule inferred solely from the details of one experimental arrangement.

**L3 core commitment**:
A value or outlook that helps explain the person's priorities, including choices under conflict or cost.
_Avoid_: A technical rule promoted only because it is frequent or old.

**Principle index**:
A categorized directory whose short descriptions help a reader select relevant principles.
_Avoid_: A combined evidence report, a complete principle library.

**Principle document**:
A self-contained explanation of one principle, including its purpose, reasoning, application, limits, and source.
_Avoid_: An unexplained slogan, a collection of several principles.
